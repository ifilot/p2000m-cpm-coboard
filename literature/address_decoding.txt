Verification notes
------------------
Factory signal ranges were checked against the P2000 M/T Field Support Manual,
pages 3-3 and 3-14. The M video board generates VIDS internally for 5000-5FFF;
therefore an FD factory-PROM byte in that range means the CPU-board RAMS2 output
is active, not that the functional memory there is extension RAM. The manual
does not publish the M PROM byte itself. The P2000T+ROM2 tables are a
project-specific variant and are not documented in the field-support manual.
